# Kola

A grandparent teaches her grandchild words in her own voice, and her Yes is the reward.

## The Unique Kernel
The real grandparent is both teacher and grader. She records the phrase, the child answers, and her tap on Yes decides whether the word joins the child's wall. No AI judges pronunciation, on purpose. A model cannot make a child want to talk to it the way he wants his grandmother's yes.

## Who It's For
A grandmother who speaks Yoruba and her grandchild who grew up speaking English and cannot answer her. Both use Android. Today she sends voice notes in her language and nobody replies in it. The Nigerian press describes this as common: children and youths in cities who cannot speak their parents' native dialects.

## The Core Loop
She records a phrase. The child opens an invite link, hears it, and records a reply. She hears the reply and taps Yes or Again. On Yes, the word appears on the child's wall. Again sends it back for another try. She comes back because a new reply is waiting; he comes back to fill the wall.

## Inspiration & Identity
Warm and personal, like a family notebook, not a school app. Her photo and his photo on the screens. Voice first, little text.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
It is the learner's own family. The elder and the child are real people who both need this to work.

## What "Working" Looks Like
On phone A she records a phrase. On phone B the child hears it and records a reply. Phone A shows the reply and she taps Yes. On phone B the word appears on the child's wall. The beat that sells it: the first time she hears him answer in her language.

## The POC Boundary
- Invite link for the child and the elder, no passwords.
- Record and play audio in the phone browser.
- One elder, one child, 6-8 starter phrases.
- Elder review screen with Yes and Again.
- The child's wall of earned words.
- Deployed so both real phones can use it.

## Later
- English meaning with tone marks.
- A shareable "first words" card.
- Offline cache.

## Explicitly Cut
- AI pronunciation scoring: the elder is the judge, by design.
- Password accounts: an invite link is enough for two people.
- Native apps: a web page works on both Android phones.
- A phrase library: she records her own phrases.
- Translation: the point is hearing her voice, not a dictionary.
